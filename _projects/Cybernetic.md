Although plants do not have brains, they possess highly sophisticated mechanisms that allow them to adapt to their environment. One of their greatest strategies is the seed. A seed is like a tiny time capsule: it can wait for months, years, or even centuries until the right moment arrives to germinate. The cells within the seed “read” environmental signals—such as moisture, temperature, and light—and in this way “decide” whether it is the right time to begin a new life. 

Its function as a biological CPU inspired the Cybernetic Seed metaphor.
